## Who We Are

Red Mesa Development is a software studio based in Calgary, Alberta. We design and build websites for local businesses, integrate AI into products, and develop full applications from the first sketch to production.

Whatever the size of the project, it ends the same way: you own the code, the accounts, and the infrastructure. No subscriptions quietly climbing every year, no vendor standing between you and your own product.

## What We Believe

- **Ownership by default.** Every product starts with the assumption that you should own it. Code, domains, accounts, model weights: all of it is handed over, documented, and yours to keep.
- **Right tool, not trending tool.** A custom-trained model, a semantic search layer, or a plain database query is often faster and cheaper than the biggest LLM available. We choose for your workload, not for a demo reel.
- **One team, no handoff friction.** Frontend, backend, AI, and infrastructure are built by the same people, so your product ships as a whole instead of a stack of pieces waiting on each other.
- **Honest, flat pricing.** You see the price before we start. Our websites are a flat $500 CAD, and the only ongoing cost is your domain renewal, paid to your registrar, never to us.
