What We Believe

The same four principles shape every project, from a three-page site to a production AI system.

Ownership by default

Every product starts with the assumption that you should own it. Code, domains, accounts, model weights: all of it is handed over, documented, and yours to keep.

Right tool, not trending tool

A custom-trained model, a semantic search layer, or a plain database query is often faster and cheaper than the biggest LLM available. We choose for your workload, not for a demo reel.

One team, no handoff friction

Frontend, backend, AI, and infrastructure are built by the same people, so your product ships as a whole instead of a stack of pieces waiting on each other.

Honest, flat pricing

You see the price before we start. Our websites are a flat $500 CAD, and the only ongoing cost is your domain renewal, paid to your registrar, never to us.
