# Red Mesa Development

AI Software Development in Calgary

Red Mesa Development is a Calgary software studio building complete products for startups, with AI integration, on-premises infrastructure, and private deployment options.

We build complete software products for startups, with AI built in and infrastructure you own.

## What We Offer

From a $500 business website to a full AI product running on your own hardware — every engagement ends with you owning the code, the accounts, and the infrastructure.

### 01 · [Website Building](/websites/) — Websites you own, not rent.

Custom-designed sites for businesses at one flat price. We build it, host it free, and hand you every key.

- Custom design, up to 3 pages, mobile responsive
- Free hosting on GitHub Pages, for life
- No subscriptions, no lock-in
- $500 flat, one-time

### 02 · [AI Implementation](/solutions/) — AI built into the product, not on top of it.

We pick the right model for the job, not the trending one, and run it on infrastructure you control.

- Voice agents with Vapi & Deepgram
- Custom-trained models and semantic search
- On-prem, private cloud, or air-gapped
- No per-token meter

### 03 · [End-to-End](/end-to-end/) — One team, idea to production.

Frontend, backend, AI layer, and infrastructure, built as one product with no handoff friction.

- New builds from design through deployment
- Equity partnerships for startups we believe in
- Support retainers as an embedded team
- Typical build: 8–16 weeks
